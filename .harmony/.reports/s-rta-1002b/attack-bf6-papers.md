# Blind-council attack papers, lane bf6 (s-rta-1002b) -- VERBATIM as relayed inline by Harmony

### SEAT STATE / CONCURRENCY attack, lane bf6 — VERDICT AMEND
A1 [MUST] The layer-clock half (Items 1d, TC5, TC6, R7) is written against a model that does not exist yet. plan-bf9b is only a skeleton, and the plan's own P0 admits it does not know how a layer finds its playing clip.
  EVIDENCE: plan-bf9b.md has 6 lines and no accessor. plan-bf6.md:274-283 (P0) and :657 (R1) concede the layer accessor is unknown. Under 'decks are boxes of clips, layers are one shared playing stack' (plan-bf6.md:136-137), the playing clip can live in a different deck's box than the layer being ticked. The current walk is `for deck: for layer: for clip` (ConnectionEngine.cpp:339-360), and plan 1d assumes `layer.getClipAt(layer.runtime().activeClipColumn)`. So the layer-knob requirement ('same with layer', Boris's item 5) has no verified design, and TC5/TC6 cannot be written or shown RED today.
  FIX: Make bf9b's accessor a hard, named contract before Stage 1: a function from the shared layer to `const Clip*` that may return nullptr, may point into another deck, and is read from ONE runtime() load. Re-derive tick's walk for the shared stack. Say whether a shared layer is ticked once or once per deck. Do not accept the plan as buildable until bf9b is merged or has frozen that signature.
A2 [MUST] Clips in non-active decks and clips not playing are still walked and ticked at 120 Hz. Their playheads are stale, and the plan does not say what a clip knob on such a clip does. The same walk has to behave correctly once decks are only boxes.
  EVIDENCE: The tick walk covers every deck, layer and clip with no active-deck or playing gate (ConnectionEngine.cpp:339-360). The playhead is written only by the GL thread's syncMedia when a clip is synced (plan E5). A clip knob on a clip that is not playing therefore follows a stale playheadPosition. Activation writes the in point (Layer.h:535-544), but a clip that never played sits at 0, and Clip::copy resets it to 0 (Clip.h:331). Item 1 changes nothing here. Probe R9 covers only an image clip. No test pins a stale or never-played video clip, and the Timeline knob on it will show a value that is not 'locked to the playhead' of anything playing.
  FIX: Decide and test it. Either a clip with no live player and not in the playing set publishes NaN (hand value), or its knob follows the stale parked position on purpose. Add a TC for a never-played video clip and for a clip left in an inactive deck.
A3 [MUST] The legacy-load upgrade (Item 3b) is a heuristic that silently rewrites meaning, and its safety rests on a scan of this machine only. A beats ramp saved at composition, macro or global-effect level becomes a permanently NaN source, so the knob stops moving.
  EVIDENCE: fromVar currently maps clock 'clipPosition' vs everything else to Beats (ConnSerialization.cpp:207-208). Item 3b rewrites 'beats, cycleBeats 4, two points (0,0)-(1,1), no envFormat' to ClipPosition, regardless of owning level (plan :236-247, :422). Composition, macro and global-effect sites get nullptr, so these knobs go NaN after the upgrade. A scan of ~/Library and ~/Documents (E13) does not cover repo fixtures, factory presets or other users' files. The plan's own CONFLICT FLAG on rulings 9/14 says 'do not migrate anything'. A lossy rewrite on load also means the next save destroys the original beat clock for good.
  FIX: Do the upgrade only where the owner is a clip or layer (a post-pass, plan option F8-B). Alternatively leave composition-level legacy ramps on Beats. Add a test: a legacy ramp at composition and macro level must still tick on beats. Ask Q4 as a gate, not as a default-yes.
A4 [MUST] The Timeline-greyed-on-non-playable rule (F5-A/Q1) contradicts Boris's literal words 'when I set ANY clip parameter to timeline it should be locked to the clips playhead'. Procedural sources, MilkDrop, cameras and images have no playhead and are 108+ of the clip kinds. The plan ships grey-out as the default.
  EVIDENCE: binding-decisions.md:591-593 says 'any clip parameter'. Plan E8 says only Video and ImageSequence are playable (Clip.h:248). F5-A greys the items out and publishes NaN. Item 5d and TC7 pin this as correct. Q1(b), a virtual playhead, is deferred behind a question.
  FIX: Treat 'what does a source/image clip's playhead mean' as an open requirement, not a default. Either a virtual clock for non-playable clips (time since trigger, bound to the clip's own bar length), or get Boris's explicit Q1 answer before building the grey-out. Do not freeze the grey-out into tests TC7, UI3a and R9 first.
A5 [SHOULD] Concurrency: a connection that reads an unlocked RelaxedDouble plus plain inPoint/outPoint across the GL/message boundary has no guard against a torn pair. The plan declares 'no shared-field type changes' and so waives TSan.
  EVIDENCE: clipTimelinePosition reads playheadPosition.load() and the raw floats inPoint/outPoint (plan :296-299). These are written on the message thread by the inspector and on the GL thread by writeBack, and the plan itself notes tsan-r5 will convert them (R10). A new GL-to-message read of a plain float that another thread writes is a new race, even though the writer already existed. G7 is only 'INFO'.
  FIX: Make G7 a gate: run probe-tsan-unit on the lane. Or read in/out through the same accessor tsan-r5 introduces, and sequence bf6 after tsan-r5 or have it share one helper.
A6 [SHOULD] The Backward playback and reverse clips double-invert, and the plan's claim of 'curve read backwards' is stated once as a design fact (:171-172) but is untested.
  EVIDENCE: clipXform maps Backward -> 1-p (plan :303-307) on top of a clip whose own reverse flag already runs the playhead down. E14 says ConnShape::playback is not reachable from the UI, so it is dead for users. But TC4 pins Backward -> 0.0 at position 1.0, a behaviour no one can reach. There is no test of reverse = true with Forward playback producing a falling value.
  FIX: Drop the Backward branch from clipXform, or label TC4's Backward half as a dead-path pin. Add a unit test with reverse = true and a falling playhead producing a falling value. R8 does this only live.
A7 [SHOULD] Layer knob continuity is unspecified at a clip change. The value jumps with no glide, and the NaN-to-value switch (image playing, then a video) snaps from the hand value to the curve.
  EVIDENCE: F2-A follows the incoming clip 'from the moment it is triggered', and F5-A publishes NaN for non-playable clips. Layer opacity on Timeline therefore snaps between the hand value (1.0) and n(p) at each cut. R2 (plan :659-662) already notes a 1-2 frame flash of curve start on a returning clip that the plan assigns to bf9b. Layer-opacity-on-Timeline over a cut is the exact case Boris asked for.
  FIX: Add a TC for a cut between a video and a still on one layer. Pin the value for the cut frame, with no one-frame spike at the curve start. Or make the returning-clip seek a hard prerequisite, not an INFO row (R13).
A8 [SHOULD] Undo and copy exactness are claimed but not tested. Clip duplicate, deck duplicate and undo of a connection-bearing edit are not exercised.
  EVIDENCE: E15 says an effect-stack undo clears grip and state. 'Timeline is a pure function ... undo is followed at once' (plan :194-195) has no test. Pitfall 36 says a duplicate re-mints clip ids. The ModelClipClock holds a raw `const Clip*` (plan :300), valid only for one tick, but nothing in the plan forbids caching it, and the layer's clock resolves a raw pointer into a vector that a message-thread edit between ticks could reallocate. There is also no round-trip test for the new marker through Clip::toVar/fromVar. TU4 checks only the legacy direction.
  FIX: Add tests: save, load and save of a new-format Timeline knob (envFormat 2 stays and is not re-upgraded); duplicate a clip or deck with a Timeline knob and tick (it follows the copy's own playhead, 0); undo an effect-stack edit and tick. State in the header that ModelClipClock is stack-only.
A9 [SHOULD] Retiring ClipPositionSignal (Item 4) is half-removal risk: saved connections, SignalBar columns and macros referencing the 'Clip Position' signal by name or id get inconsistent treatment.
  EVIDENCE: The plan makes Connection Signal('Clip Position') load as Kind::ClipPosition (3c). But SignalBar and macro bindings, which are ids, not names, silently read 0 (R8, SignalRegistry.cpp:181-189). The plan itself says picker ids are 100 + index and that 'it is the LAST entry', yet BF5 or bf45 may add signals near :63-72. An order-dependent claim is made without a test.
  FIX: Add a TR test that every other signal's registry index is unchanged. Add a load test for a SignalBar or macro with the retired id (no crash, hand value). Check that no saved id is recorded; plan :433 says ids are never saved, so state that as VERIFIED with a cite.
A10 [NIT] Several RED claims are weaker than stated: TC10, UI1, UI4 and UI6 are pins that are GREEN on main, and TC1-TC9 'RED on main' relies on null clock = 0 meaning that Item 1 itself changes.
  EVIDENCE: Plan :328-330 defines RED as 'fails on main'. TC8's 'A Beats-clock envelope on a composition scalar' passes today. Probe R3 (retrigger within 150 ms) and R4 (opacity <= 0.13 after wrap) have tolerances of about 10 ms of lag and a 120 Hz tick, and so may be flaky in the live probe.
