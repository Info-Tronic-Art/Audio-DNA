### SEAT SIGNAL/AUDIO (blind attack, lane bf2) — VERDICT AMEND
A1 [MUST] BeatLead's central bound is false. 'No hold longer than 0.5 beat + 2 hops' (I5 GREEN, T-L3) breaks on a tempo change. delta = l x bpm/60 is recomputed every hop, so a bpm drop shrinks the lead and Q falls below Q(F). Rule 3 then HOLDs, and a bpm change is not a frameEpoch re-base.
  EVIDENCE: plan-bf2.md Appendix A rules 2-3 and the delta formula. Re-base only on e != lastEpoch, bpm == 0 or a barsAdvance change; the epoch list at I5 has no tempo change. At -500 ms, 174 -> 87 bpm (a routine half-time correction) takes delta from 1.45 to 0.72 beats. Q must then catch up 0.72 beat, which is about 500 ms of frozen beatPhase/beatInBar/bar fields at 87 bpm. 200 -> 60 bpm drops 1.17 beats. T-L3 lists 'bpm jumps' but asserts the 0.5-beat bound, so either the test fails or its jump set excludes the common case.
  FIX: On a bpm change, re-base (absorb into beatX/barX) instead of holding, or scale the lead in ms-of-beat-time continuously. Add an explicit T-L3 case 174 -> 87 and 200 -> 60 at lead 500 ms with a hold bound in ms, not beats.
A2 [MUST] The acceptance rows cannot test Boris's '1 ms steps' claim. R1 and the I3 test measure the plan's own gate arithmetic. R2 measures at a 16.7 ms frame grid with D values chosen to be frame multiples.
  EVIDENCE: R1 hopDelayMs = process time minus arrival stamp, the exact quantity the gate sets, so mean(38)-mean(37) in [0.6,1.4] cannot fail unless frontDue is arithmetically wrong. It is blind to stamp jitter and to the render path. R2 lag = render pulse frameMs minus stampMs, with arms 100/250/500 ms = 6/15/30 frames at 60 Hz. The click grid is 24000 frames = 500 ms = exactly 30 frames, so every click has the same vsync phase and the frame quantisation does not average. A +-3 ms bar against a 16.7 ms quantum is a coin-flip rescued by the '2 of 3 re-run' rule. Nothing verifies the 1 ms step end to end.
  FIX: Add a sample-domain end-to-end check: inject a click into the ring with a known delivered-sample index and assert published-hop process time minus the sample-clock time of that click equals D +-0.5 ms across D = 37/38. Use non-frame-multiple D (e.g. 101, 253) in R2. Use an onset-count-based median over >=200 clicks, or compare the arms' lag distributions rather than medians.
A3 [SHOULD] Hop stamps are wall-clock arrival time at drain, not sample-clock time. D therefore inherits ring/sleep jitter and thread-stall bursts, so a 1 ms dial is below the noise floor of the mechanism.
  EVIDENCE: I3 run(): 'stamping each with juce::Time::getMillisecondCounterHiRes()' at drain. The audio callback delivers 128-sample (2.67 ms) blocks, the pull is gated by sleep(1) (AnalysisThread.cpp:89-93), and a pull happens only when a whole hop exists (AnalysisResampler.h:31-35). A stall of 20 ms stamps several hops 'now', so they are all due together and the delay collapses to a burst. The render thread then quantises to 16.7 ms anyway.
  FIX: Derive stamp_i = t0 + i x 512/48000 s (re-anchored by a slow low-pass of arrival - ideal), so hop spacing is exact. Tell Boris plainly that on-screen onsets land on the 60 Hz frame grid, so a 1 ms click shifts the statistics of landing, not any single flash.
A4 [SHOULD] Human tempo anchors (Tap, Resync) land at the press, so in manual BPM mode they cancel the room correction on the beat clock while the onset/loudness path stays D late. The two paths disagree by D after every Tap.
  EVIDENCE: F7(a) and E6/E8: requests apply at the next processed hop (BPMTracker.h:132-149; tap = setManualBPM realign, MainComponent.cpp:5616-5648). The hop processed at wall T carries audio from T-D, but the realign is stamped 'now', so beat-clock visuals sit at press time while onset-driven visuals sit at press + D. Boris asked for ONE dial that moves 'signals and bpm' together per room. R9 is only INFO and Q-list does not surface this.
  FIX: Queue tempo requests through the delay line: stamp them with press time and apply at the hop whose due time passes press + D, so Tap/Resync honour the venue offset. This keeps the Pitfall 48 seq contract because it is still applied at a hop boundary. Alternatively state it as a Boris question with a test.
A5 [SHOULD] Rate-change provenance is applied at pull time, not process time, so up to D ms of snapshots carry the wrong sourceSampleRate and spectral bandwidth.
  EVIDENCE: AnalysisThread.cpp:75-87 runs setSourceRate and spectralFeatures_->setInputBandwidthHz before pullHop. processHop (plan I3) writes snap->sourceSampleRate from resampler_.sourceRate() at :344. With 500 ms queued, the already-resampled old-rate hops get processed with the new bandwidth/rate label. The plan does not mention this Pitfall 29 interaction.
  FIX: Store the rate/bandwidth per hop in the line (two floats) and apply them in processHop. Add a test that changes rate with 40 hops queued.
A6 [SHOULD] EARLY is the most intricate piece in the plan (a 7-test state machine with hold, re-base and excess counters) for a mode Boris called rare ('we will rarely have an early delay so that is not a worry'). Its hold freezes all beat fields, contradicting the plan's own F3 principle 'never a freeze, never a jump'.
  EVIDENCE: Boris, binding-decisions.md:578-581. Appendix A rule 3 'publish F again (every beat field frozen)'. R2 in section 7 itself lists BeatLead defects as P medium.
  FIX: Ship S1a/S1b/S3/S5 with the range 0..500 first and make S2 optional after Boris tries LATE. If kept, replace holds with a bounded-rate slew of the excess (published phase rate in [0.5, 1.5]x) so counters stay monotone without freezes.
A7 [SHOULD] The only automated timing test can silently skip, so it cannot fail on the loaded machine where it matters.
  EVIDENCE: I3 RED: 'The test self-checks feeder jitter and SKIPs with a warning above 5 ms'. G1 says 'any failure blocks' but a skip is not a failure. R9 mitigation leans on it as a safety net.
  FIX: Make G1 count a skip of test_analysis_sync_thread as a block, or drive the feeder from a virtual clock. Inject the clock into SyncSlew/frontDue (the 'nowMs' parameter already exists) and run the whole gate deterministically with a fake clock.
A8 [SHOULD] Take stamps subtract the LATE offset from every point, including user gestures (keys/routine/pad presses) that are live, not delayed. Replays in a different room then fire gestures D early relative to the audio they were performed against.
  EVIDENCE: F9/I12: sampleForClock = deliveredSamples - late. RecorderHost.cpp:453-458 stamps all points with the live counter. Only snapshot-derived points (onset markers, tempo map) are D late; gestures are real-time events.
  FIX: Subtract late only for snapshot-derived stamps; keep gesture stamps live, or document and test the intended semantics with a gesture row in R7.
A9 [NIT] Startup with a saved D=500: the first-step snap reports appliedMs=500 while the line is empty, so for 500 ms the bus carries only a cleared/default snapshot and readers see no features. The slew diagnostics R3 would also read 'applied 500' on an unfilled line.
  EVIDENCE: F3(a) 'the first value at startup snaps'; I3 gate: frontDue only when stamp + D has elapsed.
  FIX: Document it, or let the first target ramp from 0 at launch. Add a check in R1 that queuedHops converges within D of launch.

### SEAT attack-bf2-live — VERDICT AMEND
A1 [MUST] In manual or tap-driven tempo, the dial stops moving the BPM clock as soon as the performer taps or resyncs. That misses Boris's 'delays or advances signals and bpm'. Plan F7 chose 'nothing special' and R9 only records it as INFO.
  EVIDENCE: applyTempoCommand (MainComponent.cpp:5616-5648) sends tap to setManualBPM (a realign) and resync to requestResync. Both apply live at the next processed hop (plan E6/F7). The grid then re-anchors at the press time, so the dial's D no longer applies to that anchor. The plan concedes this in R4: 'the dial shifts the grid only from the last anchor'. The room or projector offset is lost on the performer's most common live tempo action.
  FIX: Make tempo requests that realign (tap, resync, phase-setting) take effect at the hop whose due time is at least request time + max(D,0). To do that, stamp the request and queue it in the delay line, or apply it when the hop stamped at or after the press becomes due. Keep Pitfall 48's trackerRequestSeq semantics. Promote R9 from INFO to a blocking row, with a bar of 'downbeat appears D after the press'.
A2 [MUST] Message-thread consumers that reset live state on Resync or Tap while snapshots lag by D can produce phantom beat crossings. The plan's consumer list (E2) never audits this.
  EVIDENCE: The resync branch (MainComponent.cpp:5636-5640) zeroes beatCounter_ and beatCrossings_ immediately on the message thread. The tickFeaturePipeline quantize and beat-snap paths (:4652, :4679-4683) then diff snapshots that are still D old (up to 500 ms, about 47 hops). Pitfall 42 and Pitfall 48 exist because of this class of bug. The plan's guard lists cover only the recorder (I12).
  FIX: Add an audit row for every live-reset consumer (beatCounter_, beatCrossings_, slideshow and beat-randomize deltas :4335/:4497, quantize). Either defer each reset to the first snapshot whose trackerRequestSeq reaches the request, or add a unit test that Resync at D=500 produces no extra or lost crossings.
A3 [MUST] Several 'cannot fail' risks in the timing tests. The core LATE test can silently SKIP, and the live R1 gate measures the gate against its own clock.
  EVIDENCE: I3's test_analysis_sync_thread SKIPs when feeder jitter is above 5 ms, so a loaded CI machine reports green with nothing verified. R1's hopDelayMs is the thread's own EMA of process time minus arrival stamp. It can pass even if the audio-to-photon path is wrong, and only R2 checks end to end. R2 is paired by the stamp taken when the analysis thread drains the hop, not by sample time. The claim of '~0.1 ms resolution from the stamps' is therefore unverified. The loop sleeps in 1 ms steps and the stamp is taken when the loop notices the hop (AnalysisThread.cpp:90-93).
  FIX: Make the SKIP a hard failure on the gate machine (ctest label that fails if skipped), or drive the thread with an injected clock so the test is deterministic. Make R2 a blocking row on median shift with a stated jitter bar. Replace the '0.1 ms' claim with the measured stamp jitter.
A4 [SHOULD] EARLY mode 'HOLD' freezes every beat field for up to 0.5 beat + 2 hops, which is about 270 ms at 120 BPM. That contradicts the plan's own 'never a freeze' stance and is visible on a hard cut or a beat-snapped trigger.
  EVIDENCE: Appendix A step 3 and T-L3 ('no hold longer than 0.5 beat + 2 hops'). Boris says early use is rare, but the control exists for the 'all early' case. At that point a frozen phase, beat count and bar position stall the oscillators, envelopes and autopilot together.
  FIX: Bound the hold to at most 2 hops by re-basing sooner, using the excess counters instead of holding. If a longer hold is kept, tell Boris and drop the 'never a freeze' wording. Add a live gate that counts frozen frames at -500 in AUTO mode (R5 only asserts monotonicity).

  EVIDENCE: The control sits in the TimingWindow BPM tab, which is hidden while the SignalBar is expanded (MainComponent.cpp:2597, E14). The TopBar is over-full. Nothing else shows a non-zero offset, and the offset applies at launch with the last room's value.

A6 [SHOULD] The plan does not say what happens to the delay line when the audio source or device changes: mic to system audio to file, stop and start, or a device rate change. Up to 500 ms of the old source is still queued and gets analysed after the switch.

A7 [SHOULD] The slew trades the 'precise 1 ms' tuning feel for 2 s settle time on a large change, and a slew-down bursts hops. The plan has no gate for visible beat-locked speed change during a mid-show venue switch.
  EVIDENCE: F3 and R3: the beat rate stays within 0.70-1.30x, so the visible tempo error is up to 30 percent for 2 s. At 60 fps the render samples whole hops at 10.7 ms each, so a 1 ms step is below a frame period (16.7 ms). R3 checks only the rate band, not the visible result.

A1 [MUST] R3 slew bar (published beat rate within 0.70-1.30x per hop) cannot discriminate and will flake. The nominal slew rates are 0.75x up and 1.25x down; the bar sits only 0.05 away. Per-hop wall intervals are 14.2 ms up and 8.5 ms down. The loop sleeps up to 1 ms (plan line 263), so one hop of jitter moves the rate by about 7% and breaks the bar. The row is also ambiguous: beatPhase advances per processed hop in sample time (E6), so 'every hop advances the phase' is trivially true.   EVIDENCE: plan-bf2.md:511-513 (R3), :163 (F3 slew 0.25), :263 (sleep capped at 1 ms), :4030 witness fields :291-293. Arithmetic: 10.667/(1-0.25)=14.2 ms; 10.667/1.25=8.53 ms; bar edges 15.2 ms and 8.2 ms.
  FIX: Pre-register the metric as windowed (for example a least-squares slope of totalBeats against processMs over 200 ms), not per hop. Bar: slope 0.75x +-0.05 (up) and 1.25x +-0.05 (down). Add the endpoint check that applied reaches 500 in 2.00 s. State the divisor explicitly.
A2 [MUST] R2 median-lag-shift bar (D +-3 ms) is under-powered against the measurement quantisation. Render pulse time is quantised to 16.7 ms frames and onset detection to 10.7 ms hops. The median of about 55 quantised samples has SD of roughly 1.5 ms per arm, so a shift difference has SD of about 2 ms. A +-3 ms bar then fails by chance in a meaningful fraction of arms (3 shift arms plus the -100 arm). The 2-of-3 re-run rule then converts the noise into a pass.
  EVIDENCE: plan-bf2.md:507-510 (R2), :529-532 (decision rule 'a bar missed by no more than its own noise ... 2 of 3 PASS = PASS'). 'Its own noise' is not defined, so the bar is not pre-registered.
  FIX: Pre-register the noise floor by running 0-vs-0 arms first and measuring the SD. Set the bar from that measured SD (for example 3 x SD), or widen to +-6 ms (below one render frame). Delete the 2-of-3 loophole, or define it numerically before any run.

  EVIDENCE: plan-bf2.md:256-259 (hopDelayMs = process time minus arrival stamp), :259-260 (stamp at drain), :507-509 (lag = render pulse frameMs - stampMs), :53 (ring 341 ms).
