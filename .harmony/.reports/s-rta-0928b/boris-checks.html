Audio-DNA — what only you can check

Session s-rta-0928b (Sep 28–29). Everything below passed its automated gates; these are the feel / look checks a gate cannot make for you. Defaults stay in force until you say otherwise.

1. The app should feel lighter when nothing is happening

  • What changed: with nothing playing the app used a third of its UI thread just redrawing the whole window 30× a second (small hitches ~18 ms, 3× a second). Now the meters and waveform draw on their own. Was 17–22 ms hitches → now ~4.5 ms.
  • Check: the signal bar meters, waveform, layer playheads and beat wheel look and move exactly as before; hover, menus and tooltips over the meter bar and waveform appear instantly.
  • Tiny known quirk (macOS, same as before): after you resize the window, the folder icons in the Files grid look very slightly crisper than at startup. The old build does exactly the same.

2. Video: retrigger no longer freezes the show

  • Was: retriggering a long video to a mid-clip in-point (or a beat-snapped / cue jump, or coming back to a deck) dropped the whole output to 14 fps (1080p) or 4 fps (4K) for ~3 s, stuck on a wrong frame.
  • Now: only that layer holds its last frame for about ¼ s (1080p) or ~1 s (4K) while it catches up; everything else keeps playing. Retriggering the incoming clip mid-crossfade also just holds while the fade continues.
  • Four 4K videos at once: 36 fps → ~90 fps. Four 1080p: back to full rate.
  • Check (feel): is the ~1 s hold on a 4K mid-clip retrigger acceptable? (Most camera files catch up faster; this is the worst case, x264's default spacing.)
  • A truncated / half-copied video file no longer crashes the app — it shows as no media.

3. Image sequences use far less memory

  • Was: a 300-frame 1080p sequence kept every frame in video memory (2.4 GB); two could exhaust the machine.
  • Now: all sequences together use at most 1 GB; short loops still keep every frame. Long sequences re-load frames as they play.
  • Check: long sequences play smoothly on your real show; coming back to a deck whose sequence moved on may show the last frame for a blink. Say if a longer sequence should keep more frames.

4. Dropping media and loading never blacks out the projector

  • Was: every drop or load showed 1–14 black frames on every output (a 4K video drop ≈ 14).
  • Now: the output holds the last picture for 1–2 frames instead. Loading a composition still keeps the old show on screen until the new one is ready (making loads fully background is the next step).
  • Check: drop a 4K video mid-show on the projector — no flash.
  • A media file deleted mid-show now turns into "no media" within ~½ s (was: the next frame).
