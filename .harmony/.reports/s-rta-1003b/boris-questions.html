1. Firing clips

Already decided by you: every fire starts a video from its beginning; firing a column that is already playing starts its videos over.

  1. You paused a clip, then you fire it. A (default) It plays from its beginning. B It goes to its beginning and stays paused.
  2. A clip set to play backwards. You fire it. A (default) It starts from its end and runs back. B It starts from its beginning.

2. Dragging the playhead

Already decided by you: after you let go, the clip plays on from that point at the same speed.

  1. You keep the mouse down on the playhead and hold still. A (default) The clip keeps playing from under your hand. B The picture waits on that frame until you let go.
  2. You drop the playhead beyond a clip's end marker. A (default) It stops at the end marker and the loop starts over. B It plays the part outside the markers once.

3. BPM-synced clips

Already decided by you: a BPM box (starts at 120) and a Beats box, with x2 and /2.

  1. How the BPM box and the Beats box belong together. A (default) They are two ways of saying one thing: change one and the other follows, and the whole clip is stretched to fit. B Beats cuts the loop shorter while BPM stays.
  2. The x2 button next to Beats. A (default) It doubles the beats: the clip takes twice as long and looks slower. B It makes the clip twice as fast, like x2 next to Speed.
  3. A BPM-synced clip left running for many minutes. It follows the tempo. Over a long run it may slide off the beat a little (I have not measured how much yet). A (default) To line it up with the beat again, you fire it. B The app keeps nudging it back onto the beat by itself.

4. Cmd+Z

Already decided by you: Cmd+Z never changes what is live in the layer strip; it undoes everything else. A clip you fired is no longer an undo step.

  1. Cmd+Z and a layer that is playing. A (default) Bypass, solo, the order of the layers and effects are still undone, as today. B Cmd+Z leaves some of these alone while the layer plays — say which.
  2. You remove a layer by mistake and press Cmd+Z. A (default) The layer comes back with its clips, not playing until you fire one. B It comes back playing what it was playing.
  3. You load a deck that adds 2 layers, fire one of its clips, and press Cmd+Z. A (default) The clip keeps playing and both added layers stay. B The added layer that plays nothing goes away.

5. Sync dial

Already decided by you: every show remembers its sync, and opening a show sets the dial to it. No message says so; the SYNC button always shows the current number.

  1. Opening a show can replace tonight's number. If you never name a room, everything is on "Default". You set +55 for the room, then open a show that was saved at 0: the dial goes to 0 and the +55 is gone. A (default) That is what "every show remembers its sync" means. No way back; you set it again. B A small button in the sync panel brings back the number the show replaced.
  2. Nudging Sync from a controller. "Sync earlier" and "Sync later" can be put on a keyboard key or a controller pad. Knobs, faders and some controller buttons talk to the app a different way and cannot be put on Sync in this version (in MIDI learn the screen just keeps waiting). A (default) A key or a pad is enough. B I want it on a knob or another control — say which controller and which control.
  3. You hold a Sync key down. A (default) The number keeps moving at the Mac's key-repeat speed, like a held arrow key. B Exactly one step per press.

6. Messages

Already decided by you: the only failure message is a failed save; all other messages go.

  1. "A failed save" — which saves? A recording, a preset and an FX Save are saves too. A (default) Every save you press shows the "Save failed" box when it fails: Save show, Save Deck, Stop Recording (the take), Save preset, FX Save. B Only Save show and Save Deck. A recording, a preset or an FX Save that could not be written then shows nothing.
  2. Save Routine is refused (no take loaded, the pads are full, or the take has no bars). No file is written. A (default) Nothing shows; simply no new pad appears. B I want a sign.
  3. The yellow line "No wired mic found" / "No audio device found". A (default) It goes. A dead sound input then shows as flat meters and "---" for the tempo, with no words. B Keep that one line as the sign for "no sound input".
  4. MIDI Learn's small text "Last: Note 60 ...". A (default) It goes. B Keep it: it shows the controller is alive.
  5. The Record tab's one remaining line. A (default) Idle, it reads "Ready. Last take: (name)" — only for a take that was really saved. The counts (unresolved / moved / skipped, audio gaps) go, and so does the red "!" on a routine pad. B Something else — say what stays.
  6. Record cannot start (the audio folder cannot be written, or less than 2 GB free). A (default) Nothing shows; the button simply stays "Record Take". B I want a sign that it did not start.
