Situation: Three clips play on three layers; one of them you paused. You save the show and quit. The next day you open it.
- A (THE DEFAULT) [as today + mine]: The layers are empty, as today: a show does not keep what was playing. New: the paused clip keeps its pause and its frame; its cell shows a pause mark, and it appears on that frame when you fire it.  -- why the default: [as today + mine] The empty layers are the app's standing rule (what plays is never saved). ### Question 198 -- A safety copy after a crash   [NOT NAMED by him]
About: a crash in the middle of your work
Situation: You have worked on a show for an hour without saving. The app crashes, or the power goes.
- A (THE DEFAULT) [mine]: The app keeps a safety copy by itself every few minutes, apart from your show file. After a crash it offers it once. It never writes over your own file. The copy is offered after a crash, not at a normal quit.  -- why the default: [mine] Least surprise, and costly to undo: an hour of setting up clips, effects and mappings.
- B: As today: nothing is kept by itself. What you did not save is lost.
Why it was asked: Today the app has no safety copy, and it always opens with a new, empty show. Either way your own Save stays the only thing that changes your show file.
