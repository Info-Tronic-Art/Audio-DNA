PART 1 -- What you can do with it now (FOR BORIS)

======================================================================

1.1 What MilkDrop is inside the app

MilkDrop is one kind of "generated picture" (like Plasma or a fractal). A cell that holds it shows a dark tile with the small tag "SRC" and, under it, the programmer's code word "projectm_visualizer" (not a picture, not a friendly name). The picture is drawn by an outside library (projectM, the free MilkDrop engine), not by the app's own effects. The app has exactly ONE such engine running for the whole show: every MilkDrop cell, on every layer, is a window onto the same engine. That single fact explains most of the odd things in 1.9.

Two places to get one: (a) the "MilkDrop" tab of the browser panel (tabs: Files, FX, Sources, Compositions, Record, MilkDrop), and (b) the Sources tab, row "MilkDrop Visualizer" (under the group header "MilkDrop"): drag it onto a cell. (Corrected in the re-check: a double-click or a click on that row does NOT make a cell; the program has a handler for it that nothing ever calls.) Route (b) makes a cell with no preset attached at all, and that cell's name bar reads the code word "projectm_visualizer", not "MilkDrop Visualizer".

1.2 How a MilkDrop picture gets into a cell

1. One preset: drag a preset row from the MilkDrop tab onto a cell. The cell is named after the preset (its file name). 2. Several presets as a playlist: switch the bottom bar of the tab to "Playlist", click presets with Shift or Cmd held to pick several, then drag any of them onto a cell. The cell is named "MilkDrop Playlist (N)". 3. A whole mood group as a playlist: in the "Curated" or "All" sub-tab, drag a mood header (for example "Energetic (9)") onto a cell. 4. In both playlist ways, the three boxes of the Playlist bar (order, timing, Blend) are read at the moment of the drop and stored in that cell, even when the bottom bar is showing "Jukebox" at that moment. Changing the boxes afterwards does not change cells already made. 5. Dragging ONE row (also in Playlist mode with only one row picked) makes a single-preset cell (way 1), not a playlist. A mood group with one preset (for example "Dark (1)") makes a "MilkDrop Playlist (1)" cell that never changes preset. In the "All" sub-tab with words in the search box, a mood header drags only the presets the search left showing. Dropping onto a cell that holds something replaces it (the drop can be undone). Nothing on screen shows which preset(s) a cell holds, except its name bar: the preset's file name (single drop) or "MilkDrop Playlist (N)". The cell's inspector shows only the five sliders of 1.8 (VERIFIED: no MilkDrop or playlist word in the inspector code). IMPORTANT (see 1.9, first line): dropping a preset on a cell does NOT make that cell show that preset.

1.3 How its presets are found

  • 30 presets come inside the app (a folder inside the app, with a small list that gives each one a mood and an energy number).
  • If a folder /tmp/milkdrop-presets exists on the computer, its presets are added (a collection somebody put there by hand; on this computer today that folder does not exist).
  • One more folder of your own: Preferences, tab "Video", field "MilkDrop Presets:" (empty text "Path to .milk preset folder...") with a "Browse..." button. The app looks inside it and all its sub-folders for files ending .milk (or .prjm). A typed path that is not a folder is ignored (the field applies on Return or when you click away); an emptied field removes the folder. Setting it re-reads all the folders at once; there is no "refresh" button.
  • Everything is read once at start and again when that Preferences field changes. A preset file you add by hand while the app runs does not appear until then.
  • Presets are sorted by name in plain character order (symbols and digits first, then capital letters, then small letters; so "$$$ Royal - Mashup (189)" is first). If none are found, the list shows "No presets loaded." with a hint where to put files.
  • Mood: the 30 bundled presets get theirs from the shipped list (9 Energetic, 9 Psychedelic, 9 Geometric, 2 Calm, 1 Dark). Any other preset gets a mood GUESSED from words in its name (fire, dream, tunnel, dark ...) and, failing that, from the first letter of its name.
  • Textures that some MilkDrop presets use: the app tells the engine of no texture folder, and none ship with the app. 6 of the 30 bundled presets name a texture picture that is not built into the engine ("304", "amandio c - pulse - sth", "BEST REXIXz G AdamFX 2 ...", "Hexcollie, Krash, bdrv, EoS n aderassi - Fractal rebirth ...", "Serge + martin - crystal palace bubble006", "... tunnel001"). The engine then uses a 1-pixel stand-in or nothing for that texture (VERIFIED in the engine's source); how those 6 look is in PART 3.

1.4 How you browse and change a preset by hand (MilkDrop tab)

  • Four sub-tabs: "Curated", "Favorites", "Recent", "All". Curated and All show collapsible mood groups (Energetic, Psychedelic, Geometric, Calm, Dark, Minimal), each row with a star mark, a coloured mood dot and the name. "Favorites" is a flat list. "Recent" is the last 20 presets you picked by hand (row click, "<", ">", "?", and the random start that "Play" makes; kept only until the app closes).
  • "Curated" today is the same list as "All": it hides only presets whose energy is 0.1 or less, and no preset can have that low a number (the lowest possible guess is 0.2; the shipped list's lowest is 0.7).
  • A search box ("Search presets...") appears in the "All" sub-tab only; it narrows the groups by part of the name. The Curated sub-tab ignores it.
  • Click a row: the preset is loaded at once, with a soft fade (length = the Jukebox "Blend" slider). Because of the one engine, every MilkDrop cell changes to it.
  • Right-click a row: star or un-star it. The star is saved at once, on this computer (not in the show).
  • Row of four buttons under the sub-tabs: "<" (previous), ">" (next), "?" (a random preset; the button really shows a question mark), and "Lock". They move through the whole list in its sorted order, whatever sub-tab is showing.
  • "Lock" is NOT "keep this preset". It switches the Jukebox's automatic changing off and on (button text "Lock" when on, red "Locked" when off). See 1.9.
  • In Playlist mode a plain click on a row picks that one row (and still loads it at once); Shift or Cmd click adds or removes rows (each such click also loads that row for a look).
  • Whenever a hand pick is made, the app also makes MilkDrop the "loose picture" and clears the old loose still image: when no layer plays any clip, MilkDrop is what the preview shows. While layers play, it has no effect on the picture beyond the preset change. The loose MilkDrop picture lasts only until the next time a cell or column is fired, or a layer or the deck is cleared, while no still picture or generated-picture cell is playing: that refresh clears it (INFERRED from reading refreshPreviewFromShow; not run).

1.5 The three play modes (the bottom bar of the MilkDrop tab)

  • "Jukebox": an automatic preset changer for the whole app. Controls: a "Play"/"Stop" button; a pool box ("All", "Curated", "Favorites"); an order box ("Bag", "Random", "Sequential"); a timing box ("4 beats", "8 beats", "16 beats", "32 beats", "30 sec", "60 sec"; starts on "16 beats"); a "Blend" slider (0.5 to 5.0 s, starts 2.0 s).
  • "Play" starts it and immediately jumps to a random preset taken from the whole list (the pool box is not used for this first jump). It changes presets only on the first beat of a bar, and only while the beat is running and a MilkDrop picture is actually being drawn.
  • The boxes and the slider of the Jukebox reach the Jukebox only once the engine exists (after a first hand pick or a first MilkDrop cell drawn). A box you change before that keeps showing your choice but the Jukebox runs on its start values (All, Bag, 16 bars, 2.0 s) until you touch that box again (VERIFIED in the code: each box does nothing when the engine is not there yet).
  • Pool "Favorites" with no starred preset: at the moment of a change nothing is loaded (the timer is still reset). Pool "Curated" = "All" (1.4).
  • What the timing box really waits (by the program text; see 1.9): "4 beats" = 4 bars (16 beats); "8 beats" = 8 bars; "16 beats" = 16 bars (64 beats); "32 beats" = 32 bars; "30 sec" = 32 bars (the same as "32 beats", NOT 30 seconds); "60 sec" = 64 bars. A bar is 4 beats.
  • It also reacts to the music's structure (build-up, drop, breakdown, back to normal): on such a change it plans a switch for the next bar, but never sooner than 4 bars after the last change. On a drop it prefers an "Energetic" preset, on a build-up "Psychedelic", on a breakdown "Calm"; on normal, any. This is always on while the Jukebox plays; there is no switch for it.
  • Order: "Sequential" walks the sorted list from wherever the shared "current preset" is, and ignores the pool box and the mood choice at a drop / build-up / breakdown (it just takes the next one). "Bag" and "Random" behave the same (a plain random pick, the same preset can come twice); the program's own note says Bag is not a true bag.
  • The "Play" and "Lock" buttons do not follow each other: "Lock" switches the same changing on or off but leaves "Play"/"Stop" as it was.
  • "VJ Clip": no controls. The list itself is the control: click = load now, drag = make a cell.
  • "Playlist": the order box ("Bag", "Random", "Sequential"), a timing box ("4 beats", "8 beats", "16 beats", "32 beats"; starts on "8 beats"; these are true beats, unlike the Jukebox's list) and a "Blend" slider (0.3 to 3.0 s, starts 1.5 s). Their values are copied into a playlist cell at drop time (1.2). A line above them reads "Shift/Cmd+click to select, drag to cell", or "N selected - drag to cell" once rows are picked.

1.6 A cell's own playlist: how it plays

  • It only changes presets while (a) the cell is the clip playing on a layer, (b) it holds two or more presets, and (c) the beat is running. After the chosen number of beats it moves on and loads the next preset with a soft fade.
  • Orders: "Sequential" = next in the list. "Random" = any OTHER preset than the current one. "Bag" = a plain random pick that may repeat the same one (so, in a playlist, "Bag" can repeat and "Random" cannot).
  • The cell's very first preset is NOT loaded when the cell is fired. The playlist's first change picks the next one by the order above. The beat count is kept in the cell between fires (nothing resets it when the cell is fired again, only a clear of the cell), so after a re-fire the first change can come sooner than N beats; likewise the place in the list is kept (VERIFIED by finding no reset in the code).
  • The saved file can also say "change every N bars", "every N phrases" (16 beats each), "on a drop", "on a breakdown", or "manual". None of these has any control on screen; they work only if the saved show file is edited by hand. The playlist's own "Blend" number is saved but not used (1.9).

1.7 What follows the beat and what does not

  • The picture itself moves to the SOUND, not to the app's tempo: the engine gets the live sound (a short piece of the mono input, every frame) and does its own beat finding. The only slider that clearly tunes this is "Beat Sensitivity" (0 to 2 internally; the slider is 0 to 1, starts in the middle).
  • The app's tempo and bar count drive only the changing of presets: the Jukebox (bars), a cell's playlist (beats), plus the Jukebox's reaction to build-up / drop / breakdown. Stop the beat and both stop changing presets (the picture keeps moving to the sound).
  • A MilkDrop cell is not "playable" in the app's sense: it has no play head, so the pause/stop of the clip transport does not freeze it, and "End of Video" autopilot cannot end it (the clip autopilot can still count beats and move on).
  • The slider "Speed" is wired to the engine's "how long a preset stays" number (5 to 60 seconds, the more Speed the shorter). Re-checked in the engine's own source (VERIFIED): when that time is up the engine only sends a "switch now" message that the app never listens to, so it changes NO preset. The only thing the number changes is a value "progress" (0 to 1 over that time) that a preset's own equations may read; none of the 30 bundled presets reads it. So "Speed" shows no effect on the bundled presets (a preset from your own folder might use it).
  • The global master speed of the show does not speed MilkDrop up or slow it down (the MilkDrop draw ignores the time it is given; VERIFIED in the code). The engine also does no "hard cut" on its own (it is off by default in the engine and the app never turns it on).

1.8 The five sliders of a MilkDrop cell, and what is kept

Sliders in the cell's inspector (all start at the middle): "Beat Sensitivity", "Speed", "Warp Amount", "Decay", "Gamma". Like other sliders, they can be connected to audio signals or controls. Only "Beat Sensitivity" is read and acted on with certainty; "Warp Amount", "Decay" and "Gamma" are shown but nothing reads them (1.9); "Speed" see 1.7.

What is saved WITH THE SHOW, per MilkDrop cell: its name, the five slider values and connections, the list of presets (each as a full file location, plus name), the order, the timing number (and unit), the Blend number and an "enabled" flag. What is NOT saved with the show: which preset is showing now; the Jukebox (on/off, pool, order, timing, Blend: all return to their start values at each launch, Jukebox off); the Recent list; the mood list. Saved on THIS COMPUTER, outside any show: your stars (a file "milkdrop_userdata.json" in the Audio-DNA folder of the user's Library; they are kept by preset NAME) and the extra folder from Preferences (in the app's settings file). Opening a show does not touch the engine: it keeps showing what it showed. When the engine is first drawn after a launch (or drawn again after the preview's drawing was torn down and rebuilt, for example the preview panel hidden and shown again: INFERRED), it starts on the app's "current preset": the first in the sorted list (today "$$$ Royal - Mashup (189)") or your last hand / Jukebox pick, never what a cell's playlist last showed. If a preset file is missing or broken, the engine keeps the previous preset (and shows its own built-in idle picture if it never had one); nothing on screen says so (VERIFIED in the engine's source).

1.9 What the keyboard, MIDI, OSC and the web interface can reach

  • No key, MIDI note or CC, OSC address or web-interface call is about MilkDrop presets. Nothing in the key, MIDI, OSC, binding or web-interface code mentions MilkDrop (the one word "preset" found there means saved sets of bindings).
  • What they CAN do is generic: fire a cell (a MilkDrop cell like any cell), and drive the cell's sliders (a slider can be connected to a signal or written by a control).
  • The web interface (port 7070) has one generic call "load_source" that can set the "loose picture" to the MilkDrop source (it has no preset field, and its parameter values do not reach MilkDrop's sliders). Only the test interface (port 8080, for testing, not for the show) has a call that loads a preset.

1.10 What is odd or broken now (each line one sentence; labels as in PART 2/3)

1. Dropping a preset on a cell does not load it: the cell's saved preset is not used when the cell is fired, so the cell shows whatever preset the one engine happens to have loaded (VERIFIED by search: nothing but the playlist timer loads a cell's presets). 2. Two MilkDrop cells on two layers always show the same picture, and two playlists on two layers fight over the one engine, last change wins. 3. The Jukebox timing list lies: its words say beats and seconds, the engine counts bars times four, and "30 sec" equals "32 beats" (this is already R212, not named by you). 4. A cell's playlist "Blend" slider is saved but does nothing; the fade length always comes from the Jukebox "Blend" slider (R202 c). 5. "Warp Amount", "Decay" and "Gamma" sliders do nothing (their lines in the code are empty or missing); "Speed" changes no preset and, with the 30 bundled presets, nothing visible (1.7). 6. "Lock" does not lock the current preset: it turns the Jukebox changing on or off, and it reads "Lock" at launch although the Jukebox is off; the real "keep this preset" switch exists in the code but nothing in the app uses it. 7. Pressing "Play" before any MilkDrop has been drawn or clicked turns the button to red "Stop" and does nothing, because the Jukebox is not yet connected to the engine. 8. A Jukebox or playlist change never shows in "Recent", never moves the highlight in the list and never updates the file label; only hand picks do. 9. The Jukebox only counts bars while a MilkDrop picture is being drawn; if other clips cover the layers, it stands still. 10. Changing the folder in Preferences re-reads the presets and forgets, in memory, your stars and the shipped moods until the next launch, and the next star you set then overwrites the saved stars with the shortened list (INFERRED from the code path; not run). 11. "Curated" is the same list as "All", and "Bag" is the same as "Random" in the Jukebox. 12. A saved cell stores full file locations, so a show moved to another computer or to a differently placed app points to files that may not be there (INFERRED; not run). 13. The playlist triggers by bar, phrase, drop and breakdown have no control and work only from an edited file. 14. In the cell tile, the word shown is the code word "projectm_visualizer". 15. The Sources-tab "MilkDrop Visualizer" cell has no preset at all and never gets one on its own; its name bar also reads "projectm_visualizer"; and a click or double-click on that row does nothing (only the drag works). 16. Your own words about labels are NOT yet built: BD:675 reads, in the harmony text, Q12 MilkDrop preset change labels — Boris: "bars". and BD:648-649 has his "I have beats and seconds in the milkdrop editor. I only need beats."; the lists still say beats and seconds. 17. A Jukebox choice made before the engine exists is not passed on (1.5), and "Play" and "Lock" do not follow each other. 18. A playlist cell's beat counter and place in its list are not reset when the cell is fired again, so the first change after a re-fire is unpredictable (1.6). 19. Six of the 30 bundled presets name textures the app never supplies (1.3); how they look is not known. 20. A mood group with no preset is not shown at all: with the bundled set there is no "Minimal" group, "Dark" has one preset and "Calm" two. (A plain fact, not a fault.)
