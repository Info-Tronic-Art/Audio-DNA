What a take is
5. VERIFIED (via FT section 1; RM:7-13). A take = take.json in ~/Documents/Audio-DNA/Takes/<name>.adna-take/ (format "audiodna-take", v3): meta (recordedAt, duration, durationBeats, startBeatInBar), an audio REFERENCE, tempoMap, checkpoint0 (the look at Record), checkpointEnd, markers, lanes. No picture is stored. No show is named: a replay is matched against whatever show is open (RecorderHost.cpp:786).
6. VERIFIED (RM:9). The audio lives in the Audio Store ~/Documents/Audio-DNA/Audio/<id>.adna-audio/{audio.wav, audio.json}; many takes can point at one audio; the app never deletes stored audio (BD:520-521); the location is fixed (BD:524-526).
7. VERIFIED. Disk guard: a take with audio refuses to start under 2 GB free (AudioTap.h:138, AudioTap.cpp:109; RecorderHost.cpp:235 "audio recording could not start; the disk needs at least 2 GB free"); the floor is checked at arm, not during the take (AudioTap.cpp: the only caller of freeSpaceBytes is :109).
8. VERIFIED (via FT section 1-2). A take records exactly ten kinds of event (clip / column fire, auto-play, deck switch, clear clip, tempo command, audio transport, layer flags, effect bypass, clip play/pause) plus continuous values that arrive through the manualWrite funnel (REST set_param, OSC, MIDI, key and pad bindings). It does NOT record a slider moved with the mouse, the layer strip's B and S buttons, or a preset load.
9. VERIFIED (RM:13). A take's t = 0 is the first 120 Hz tick whose snapshot carries every tempo command sent before Record; a take started while the tracker is unlocked is an "unmetered start" and a routine cut at its beat 0 is refused "by design".
Replay today
10. VERIFIED (FT section 1; RecorderHost.cpp:818, :571). "Play Take" compiles the take against the LIVE show, restores checkpoint 0 (the look at Record), then writes the recorded moves into the live model each tick: the replay IS the live layers and the live outputs. It always starts at 0; there is no jump or scrub (Player::seek and a backward advanceTo exist, no host-level jump).
11. VERIFIED (RM:11). At the take's end the replay HOLDS (stays "playing", finished, position pinned) and, with audio, gives the live input back; "Stop Playback" is the exit. Not restored: video playheads, crossfade progress, pending triggers, tempo, audio transport.
12. VERIFIED (RM:11). "Play with audio" replays the stored sound through the transport; without it the replay is silent and runs on the wall clock.
