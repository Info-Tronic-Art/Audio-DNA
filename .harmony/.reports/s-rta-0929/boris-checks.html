Audio-DNA — what only you can check (session s-rta-0929)

Everything below passed the automated tests. These checks need your eyes, your hands, or real hardware. Tell me what you see; nothing blocks on them.

1. Loading a composition no longer freezes the app

  • Load a composition that has many video clips (File > Open, or the library). The window should not freeze. The label reads "Loading <name>..." for a moment, and the output keeps playing the current show until the new one cuts in, with no black flash.
  • Press keys or pads during that moment: they should still act on the show you are looking at.
  • Right-click a deck tab > Duplicate twice quickly: you should get two copies, the second one active.
  • Choose Load Deck... while a composition is still loading: the deck should be added after the composition arrives.

2. Several videos at once stay smooth

  • Trigger a whole column of four 1080p video clips at once: the preview should stay smooth (it now holds 120 fps on the laptop display; before, it could drop to ~105).
  • Four 4K videos together should also feel smoother than before (about 100-104 fps, was 85-90).
  • Play a clip in reverse and in ping-pong: the motion should look as smooth as before.
  • While a video clip plays, minimise and restore the main window, or unplug and replug a projector. The layer must never flash to "effects only" (the picture should stay). A real graphics-driver reset cannot be produced on the test rig.

3. A playing routine uses less CPU, looks the same

  • Play a routine on a few layers: the routine pad, the chartreuse bands and the faders should look and move exactly as before. Only how often the pad redraws changed (about 12 times a second instead of 30).
