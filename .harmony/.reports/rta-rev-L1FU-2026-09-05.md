## Q1 — Is the hazard actually closed? YES, verified.
Traced: openVideoForClip/openImageSequenceForClip now call closeMediaForClip(clipId)
after player->open()/seq->open() succeeds, before the map assignment
(Renderer.cpp:998, 1017 in working tree). closeMediaForClip finds the old entry,
calls it->second->close() (CPU/FFmpeg-only, thread-safe), then push_back's the
unique_ptr into retiredVideoPlayers_/retiredImageSequences_ and erases the map
entry (Renderer.cpp:1024-1057) — no destructor runs here. Actual destruction is
deferred to drainRetiredMedia() (Renderer.cpp:1059-1079), called every frame from
renderOpenGL() and once from openGLContextClosing(), both GL-thread-guaranteed
per JUCE contract. ~VideoPlayer() unconditionally calls releaseGL()->glDeleteTextures
(VideoPlayer.cpp:26-29,344-348, confirmed by direct read) — this is exactly what
the old bare `map[id]=move(new)` would have triggered synchronously on the message
thread; now it only ever destructs after drainRetiredMedia's explicit releaseGL()
on the GL thread. Hazard closed for both named functions.

Additional site swept and found SAFE with reasoning not in the packet: `~Renderer()`
(Renderer.cpp:18-21) calls detach()->glContext_.detach(), which runs
openGLContextClosing() synchronously on the GL thread per JUCE's contract BEFORE

in-place destruction on the message thread, ~VideoPlayer()/~ImageSequence()'s
releaseGL() calls are harmless no-ops. Not a hazard; not touched by this diff.

## Q4 — Locking: correct, no new hazard.
`closeMediaForClip(clipId)` is called at both new call sites with NO mutex held
(the local `lock_guard` on videoPlayerMutex_/imageSeqMutex_ is declared AFTER the
new call, per the diff). Internally, closeMediaForClip acquires/releases
videoPlayerMutex_+retiredMediaMutex_ (nested, scoped) then separately
imageSeqMutex_+retiredMediaMutex_ (nested, scoped) — never holds both
videoPlayerMutex_ and imageSeqMutex_ simultaneously, no lock-order inversion.
drainRetiredMedia() releases retiredMediaMutex_ (swap-out pattern) BEFORE calling
releaseGL() — confirmed no GL call happens under that mutex. (Pre-existing,
untouched-by-this-diff note: openGLContextClosing() DOES hold
videoPlayerMutex_/imageSeqMutex_ while calling releaseGL() at lines 731-743 — GL
call under a mutex — but this is L1's code, not this lane's, is GL-thread/
context-teardown-only, and was already reviewed at L1. Not a new issue.)
