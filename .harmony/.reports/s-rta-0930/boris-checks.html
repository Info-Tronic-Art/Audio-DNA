Audio-DNA — what only you can check

Shipped this session: reverse and ping-pong video now stay smooth when the reverse memory is capped. Four 1080p clips reversing together in a 256 MB budget went from 100 late frames to 0, at about 30 frames per second each. A single 4K clip reversing in a 512 MB budget went from 2.4 to 13.8 frames per second. Clips whose keyframes are spread across frames (intra-refresh H.264) now reverse from memory instead of re-seeking over and over. Every automated check I ran passed; the items below need your eyes, or your call.

Planned, not built yet (ready for next session): the fix for the 20 thread races (one could flash a wrong opacity for a frame on a clip trigger), and the Bluetooth-guard follow-ups (fewer audio device re-opens at launch; picking up a wired mic you plug in after launch when the app had none). Fable was out, so Opus 5.5 wrote the plans, as you said.

1. Feel checks (only if you want to)

  • Four 4K clips in one column, all set to Reverse, trigger the column: they used to barely move (~2 frames a second each); now they should move (~12–14 each), about half as smooth as forward.
  • Any reversed clip you know well, in a column of four: it should look exactly as before and never stall. The change only alters when frames are decoded.
  • If you use screen recordings or game captures: reverse one. Fast parts show fewer frames than forward. That is by design (question 1).

2. Your calls (each has a default; nothing waits on you)

  1. Screen recordings / phone clips with dropped frames in reverse: fast parts show fewer frames than forward (0% for normal phone video, up to about half for a screen recording). Fix now (a bigger change to the video engine) or only if you notice it? Default: documented now, fixed on request.
  2. Four 4K clips reversing together: they move now but are not smooth with software decoding. OK for now? Default: yes; hardware decoding stays on the list.
  3. Firing a column that is already playing: today it does not restart its videos, but clicking a single playing cell does. Should firing the same column again restart every clip in it? Default: keep today's behaviour.
  4. Column switch timing: when you fire a whole column, one layer can switch one frame (1/120 s) before the others. Should all layers switch on exactly the same frame, even if that makes some switch a frame later? Default: keep today's timing (lowest latency).
  5. Momentary pad with Quantize on: press and let go before the beat arrives. Today the clip still starts on the beat and then stays on. Should letting go before the beat cancel it? Default: yes (the code's own notes say that was the intent).
  6. Wired mic plugged in while the app is on the MacBook mic (because your wired mic dropped out mid-show, or you plugged it in after launch): (a) stay on the MacBook mic until you relaunch; (b) switch to the wired mic by itself within about a second; (c) for a drop-out only, never fall back to the MacBook mic: show the "plug one in" note and wait. Default: (a), never switch away from a mic that works.
  7. The "mic lost" note: if your wired mic drops out and the app moves to the MacBook mic by itself, a yellow note says Mic "<your mic>" lost - now listening on "MacBook Pro Microphone". and stays until you relaunch or the mic changes again. Keep that note or drop it? Default: keep it.
