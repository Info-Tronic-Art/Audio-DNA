   Compared directly against the sibling `writeSilenceFrames()` (`:246-258`), which explicitly does

   `numSamples` exceeds the last-prepared `maxBlock_` while `chans < channels_` does read past
   `silenceStorage_[0]`'s allocation. The report's line numbers (`AudioTap.cpp:239-243`, `:246-258`)
   match current disk content exactly.
