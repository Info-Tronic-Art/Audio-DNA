Audio-DNA — the deck change is in your app (Oct 3, evening)

Nothing waits on you. This page is only for looking and saying what feels wrong.

1. What is different in your app now

  • Decks are boxes of clips. The layers hold what plays. Switching decks changes only the grid — never the picture.
  • The layer strips show the clip that is playing. No deck number on a strip, no dot on a deck tab.
  • Delete a deck while one of its clips plays: the clip keeps playing. No button, no message. Cmd+Z brings the deck back.
  • "Persistent" is gone from the Layer tab. "Ignore Column Trigger" stays.
  • The top bar has no "Fade:" (a deck switch has nothing to fade).
  • MilkDrop keeps playing, and keeps changing presets, while you browse other decks.
  • Under the hood: a memory fault that could hit when a wide deck was loaded is fixed; 33 test scripts can no longer quit an app they did not start (after yesterday's incident).

2. Check it — about 5 minutes

A test show is ready: in the app, Composition → Load… and pick

20 decks. Every clip is a picture with big letters like "D3 C2" (deck 3, column 2). Deck 1 column 4 is a video with a running clock.
  1. On Deck 1 click "D1 C1" in the Layer 1 row and the clock video (column 4) in the Layer 3 row. Click the Deck 3 tab and click "D3 C2" in the Layer 2 row. Now click through all 20 deck tabs and back.

  2. While you click through the tabs, watch the layer strips on the left.
    Expect: they do not change at all. No number, no dot. On Deck 3 the "D3 C2" cell is lit; on other decks no cell in that row is lit — that is correct.
  3. Select Layer 2, open the Layer tab, tick "Ignore Column Trigger". Click the Deck 5 tab, then the column number 4 above the grid.
    Expect: Layer 2 keeps D3 C2; Layers 1 and 3 show D5 C4.
  4. Untick it. Click the Deck 6 tab and the column number 2 (Deck 6 has nothing in the Layer 2 row there).
    Expect: Layer 2 goes empty; Layers 1 and 3 show D6 C2.
  5. On Deck 3 click "D3 C2" in the Layer 2 row, click the Deck 1 tab, then right-click the Deck 3 tab → Remove Deck.
    Expect: D3 C2 keeps playing, the Deck 3 tab is gone, nothing is announced, and the Layer tab still shows your layer. Cmd+Z: the tab is back, still playing.
  6. Layer tab: "Persistent" is gone; "Ignore Column Trigger" sits alone on its row.
  7. Open your own show "test with harry".
    Expect: your layers look as they did. No note appears.
  8. Deck menu → Load Deck… → five-rows.json in the same folder (a deck with five rows; the show has three layers).
    Expect: a new deck tab, two more layer rows, the picture does not change. Cmd+Z takes the deck and the two rows away again.
