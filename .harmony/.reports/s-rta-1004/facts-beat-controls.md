## 1 QUESTIONS ANSWERED

### Q1 Every control that sets the tempo or moves the beat phase today

Answer in plain words: eight things set tempo and/or phase: Tap, Resync, the Manual toggle, the typed BPM field, Ableton Link, REST (set_bpm, resync), OSC (/audiodna/bpm, /audiodna/resync) and two bindable actions (Tap Tempo, Resync; a key or a MIDI note/CC can be bound to them). The "/4 /2 x1 x2 x4" buttons look like tempo controls but are NOT: nothing reads their value (see below). All of them funnel into one message-thread function, `MainComponent::applyTempoCommand`, which only posts a request; the analysis thread applies it at the start of its next hop (~10.7 ms). Two kinds exist: a beat GESTURE (Tap, Resync: realign the phase) and a tempo VALUE (typed BPM, REST/OSC set_bpm, Link: change the rate only, never the phase).

### Q2 Is there any nudge (small step earlier/later without changing the tempo)?

No. VERIFIED by reading every tempo/phase writer (Q1) and by grep for nudge/phase-offset/beat-shift in src: no control, binding target, REST route, OSC address or MIDI action moves the beat by a chosen small amount. The only phase-moving actions are hard realigns to 0 (Tap, Resync, a confident detection), all of which snap "now" to the beat, never "N ms earlier/later". The only "phase offset" controls in the app are the per-signal oscillator/envelope phase sliders in the Signal inspector (src/ui/SignalInspector.cpp:66-72, 115): they shift one LFO/envelope's shape, not the shared beat clock, not the bar counter, and not clip quantise, routines, autopilot or slideshows. The earlier "sync dial" (BF2) is unmerged and not in main (binding-decisions.md "REPLACES"); there is also no output latency/delay setting in src (grep "latency" finds only unrelated video/decode comments).

### Q3 What a user does TODAY to make the shown beat agree with the heard beat, and what he cannot do

Steps with real control names (VERIFIED as to what each control does; INFERRED as to how well it works by ear):
1. Top bar, choose the audio source (the "Audio" selector). In Auto the tempo number turns green with the word LOCKED once the tracker has a lock; red SEARCHING / yellow LOCKING otherwise (TopBar.cpp:339-363).
2. If the tempo is wrong (half/double, or off): click **Manual**, type the number in the BPM field and press Enter (value only, the beat keeps running; it is folded into 60..200), or click **Tap** in time with the music (at least twice; the 3rd..8th tap refine the average; > 2 s gap restarts).
3. To put the beat's "1" on the music's "1": click **Resync** exactly on the downbeat you hear. That makes beat phase 0, beat-in-bar 0, bar 0 at that instant (applied within ~one 10.7 ms analysis hop). Each Tap also realigns the phase to 0 (but not beat-in-bar).
4. To do it without the mouse: Shortcuts > Edit Keyboard Shortcuts... or Edit MIDI Mappings..., pick the "Tap Tempo" or "Resync" target in the overlay, press a key or a MIDI note (MenuBarModel.cpp:153-162; MainComponent.cpp:7578-7582). Nothing is bound by default.
5. For a tempo that Manual holds, the beat then free-runs at that rate (detected beats are ignored). In Auto the detector re-aligns on each confident onset by itself.

What he cannot do today (VERIFIED unless noted):
- Move the beat earlier or later by a small, repeatable amount while the tempo stays (Q2). The only way to "shift" is to click Resync/Tap again at the right instant; the precision is the user's click timing plus up to one analysis hop (~10.7 ms, INFERRED from BPMTracker.cpp:74-84 and the Pitfall 48 comment) plus UI/render latency (not measured).
- Shift the beat relative to the picture independently of the sound: the top-bar beat display is drawn from the same snapshot as everything else (Q4), so there is no "match the image" offset.
- Use /2 or x2 to fix a half/double-time tempo (the buttons do nothing). The workaround is to type or tap the intended tempo. Note a typed 30 becomes 60 and 400 becomes 200 (fold), and the tempo label shows the typed text, not the folded value (TopBar.cpp:321-329).
- Turn Manual off from REST/OSC (there is none), or tap from REST/OSC (none).
- See, in the UI, a number for how far off the beat is.

### Q5 What "the beat" drives

Everything below reads the analysis tracker's clock through `FeatureSnapshot` (`beatPhase`, `beatInBar`, `barPhase`, `phrasePhase`, `barCount`, `totalBarCount`, `totalBeatCount`, `resyncBarOrigin`), published by AnalysisThread.cpp:203-214. A planner moving "the beat" moves all of these at once unless a design splits them. Table T2 in section 2 lists each consumer with what it reads. Key points:
- VERIFIED: beat-CROSSING consumers integrate the `totalBeatCount` delta (Pitfall 42), never the `beatPhase` wrap. A hard realign from the second half of a beat adds +1 to totalBeatCount (BPMTracker.cpp:253-258), so a Tap/Resync late in a beat fires one beat edge immediately: every beat-edge consumer (autopilot, slideshow, beat-sync randomize, routines, quantised-clip fire, playlist) sees that edge. A phase-only nudge in the first half of the beat would add 0.
- VERIFIED: Resync additionally resets the bar grid (beatInBar, barCount, phrase) and sets `resyncBarOrigin`; Tap realigns the beat phase only.
- VERIFIED: tempo VALUES never touch the beat count or phase (BPMTracker.h:149-158; Pitfall 42 text).

## 3 WHAT A PLANNER MUST NOT ASSUME

1. The "/4 /2 x1 x2 x4" buttons are decoration: they store `bpmMultiplier` in the composition file and nothing reads it. A line "x2 and /2 change the tempo" would be wrong. (Boris's question list assumes "x2 and /2 if any".)
2. docs/claude/effects.md "Manual BPM Mode" is right that a typed BPM changes tempo only, but it does not say that Tap does NOT enter Manual, that REST/OSC set_bpm does force Manual on without moving the TopBar Manual toggle (INFERRED), or that Manual-on applies the field's old text or 120 rather than the detected tempo shown a moment later.
3. Auto mode undoes a manual realign: a confident detected beat resets the phase to 0 itself (BPMTracker.cpp:234-236). A "move the beat" control that only offsets phase_ would be overwritten at the next confident onset in Auto; in Manual the detector is ignored (BPMTracker.cpp:99-104).
4. The tap code exists twice (TopBar button and bound key at MainComponent.cpp:7821), each with a fixed 8-slot array. After the 8th tap `n` stays at 8, so taps 9, 10, ... recompute the same BPM from taps 1..8 (`tapTimes_[0]` and `[7]` never change) yet each still realigns the phase to 0 (TopBar.cpp:62-77; derived from the code, not run).
5. totalBeatCount side effects: any hard realign from the second half of a beat adds +1 and so fires a beat edge for every counter consumer at once; from the first half it adds 0 (Pitfall 42; BPMTracker.cpp:253-258). Resync also moves bars/phrases/oscillator shapes (`resyncBarOrigin`); Tap does not move beat-in-bar, so after a Tap the wheel's lit segment and `totalBeatCount` can disagree about which beat of the bar it is (INFERRED from BPMTracker.cpp:253-258, 280-286).
6. Pitfalls that bind this area (docs/claude/pitfalls.md, CLAUDE.md index): 30 (beat-like events: act on counter deltas, not one-hop flags), 32 (`downbeatDetected` is a beat-long LEVEL; Resync sets it true for the whole first beat), 38 (one Autopilot per frame; never call `processFrame` twice), 42 (a beat clock integrates `totalBeatCount + beatPhase`, never the wrap; test-mode injection must move `totalBeatCount` with `beatPhase`), 48 (a tempo command reaches the snapshot only at the next hop; "everything sent before X" = `trackerRequestSeq >= postedRequestSeq()`). Sacred Rules 1-4 (CLAUDE.md): nothing may be added to the audio callback; any new beat-offset field the render/UI threads read must travel by the established channels (Relaxed<T> / FeatureBus), no new mutex.
7. The beat display refreshes at 15 Hz (TopBar) and 30 Hz (readout), so it cannot show a few-ms offset visually (VERIFIED rates).
8. Link looks like a beat-sync control but only follows tempo; the app's beats are not aligned to other Link peers' beats (performance-controls.md:76, matches code). Default builds ship with Link off.
9. "Manual" label in the UI is a tracker mode, not a beat-offset mode; there is no existing "offset" field in FeatureSnapshot (grep: none); the signal inspector's "phaseOffset" sliders are per-signal and not the shared beat.
10. design/ux_analyses/Resolume/.../help/*.xml is Resolume's help, not the app's; README.md is a developer/build readme, not a user manual; "About Audio-DNA" opens Preferences, not an About page.
11. The sync dial / BF2 (earlier-later nudge) is NOT in main; do not treat it as an existing nudge.

### C. What the sheet leaves out that a planner needs

1. Test/inject paths (above): ApiServer.cpp:957-1045 and TestServer.cpp:495-525 set beatPhase/beatInBar/totalBeatCount directly; any "offset" field in FeatureSnapshot has to decide what these do.
2. Where the offset would live and which readers it must reach. The sheet says "moves all at once unless a design splits them" but does not list the readers that bypass the tracker's output fields: PresetSelector uses a barPhase WRAP (not the counter); the beat-snap seek copies beatPhase into a playhead (MainComponent.cpp:4866-4885); shaders and mapping read beatPhase/barPhase/phrasePhase directly; the take recorder builds its own continuous beat from totalBeatCount + beatPhase (RecorderClock.cpp:15). An offset applied to beatPhase alone, without totalBeatCount, would make these disagree; the sheet names the +1 rule but not this multi-field consistency requirement as a design constraint.
3. A moved beat with onset-driven visuals: effects driven by `onsetDetected` / `onsetStrength` / bands (the audio itself) do not move when the beat clock moves. Boris's "match the image" may refer to the beat-clock-driven part only; the sheet does not say which visuals follow the clock and which follow the raw audio.
4. Audio-to-ear latency. The sheet says no latency setting exists but not where the sound a user hears comes from: AudioEngine.h:21 says file mode is "no playback output" (analysis only), so the heard sound comes from another player or the system/mic path whose latency the app cannot see; no `getOutputLatency`/input latency query exists in src. This decides whether a fixed offset (calibration) or a per-song hand trim is the right control.
5. Precision numbers for a design: hop = 512 samples @ 48 kHz = 10.67 ms (a tempo/phase request lands only on a hop boundary); the displayed wheel steps at 67 ms. A nudge finer than the hop is not representable if applied inside the tracker per hop, but is if applied at read time. The sheet states the hop (INFERRED) but not that a fraction-of-hop offset needs a read-side design.
6. Persistence: nothing says where such a setting would be saved (settings.json vs composition vs take). The sheet notes Link state is not saved; bpmMultiplier IS saved in the composition but inert -- a possible home or a trap for a new control.
7. Replay: a take/routine replays "tempo" actions (MainComponent.cpp:1975-1981) with Origin::Replay; a new offset action would need a capture/replay decision (the existing five are captured).
8. Manual toggle on a REST/OSC set_bpm: the sheet marks the TopBar toggle not updating as INFERRED; I found no setter either (only `manualMode_` written in the toggle's onStateChange, TopBar.cpp:100-119), so the static read agrees, still unrun.
9. Auto mode: BPMTracker.cpp:235-236 realigns on every confident beat, so in Auto an offset implemented as a one-shot phase shift is erased within about a beat. The sheet says this (B3 item 3) but does not say whether the intended manual ("put it in our manual") should tell the user to switch to Manual first.
