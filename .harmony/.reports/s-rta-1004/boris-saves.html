Audio-DNA — everything the app saves

Why this page. You wrote: "we should list all the things that can be saved". This is that list.
It was read out of the code of the app you run today, by three separate searches, merged, then checked again by a fourth reader who looked for anything missing and found nothing. Nothing here is built or changed — it is the app as it is.
A line marked * was read once, not twice.

A. Things you save yourself

  • The show — Save, or Save As with a new name. The routines on the routine pads live inside it.
  • A deck — Save Deck, or Save Deck As with a new name.
  • Collect Media — copies your pictures and videos next to a copy of the show.
  • An effects look — the small Save button, to a name you choose.
  • FX Save — the quick effects save, numbered by itself.
  • Your key and MIDI settings — only through Export. This is the only place they are kept (see "Things you may not expect").
  • The window layout — Save Layout.
  • A snapshot — a still picture of the screen.
  • Save Routine — kept only inside the show, so the show must be saved too.

B. Recordings

  • A show recording (a take) — every move you make, plus the sound. The app writes it when you press Record, again when the tempo is known, every minute, and when you stop. If you quit while it records, it stops and saves it.
  • The sound of a take — its own sound file beside the take.
  • A video recording of the screen — Output > Start Recording. A video file, picture only, no sound*.
  • Repair Audio — mends the sound record of a take that was cut off.

Recording video into a cell is not in the app yet.

C. Things the app keeps by itself (no save pressed)

  • Which output screens were open, so "Restore Last Outputs" can bring them back.
  • The MilkDrop folder you picked.
  • Your MilkDrop favorites.
  • The files and folders you starred in the file browser.

That is all of it. Four things.

Not kept at all

  • Key and MIDI settings (unless you export them).
  • The audio device you picked.
  • Manual tempo and the BPM you typed.
  • Link on or off.
  • The MIDI output choice.
  • The tooltips choice.
  • The window size.

The app never saves the show by itself, and today it does not ask anything when you quit with an unsaved show.

Things you may not expect

  1. Your key and MIDI settings are gone when you quit, unless you exported them and import them again. The app does not remember them by itself. See question 50 below.
  2. Save Routine says it saved, but the routine lives only inside the show. Quit without saving the show and the routine is gone.
  3. A video recording can fail silently. The app always reports it as finished, even when the file could not be written.
  4. The audio device you pick is not remembered between launches.
