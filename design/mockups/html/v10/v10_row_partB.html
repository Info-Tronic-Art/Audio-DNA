AUDIO-DNA V10 -- LAYER ROW DESIGN STUDY PART B (V11-V20)
Signal Reinvented (V11-V14) and Layout Radical (V15-V20) explorations. Each variation shows a full layer row: strip + cells grid at 1200px reference width. No K slider anywhere. Speed and signal placements vary per design strategy. Part A (V1-V10) in separate file.
CATEGORY C -- SIGNAL REINVENTED
V11: SIGNAL-EDGE-BARS-ON-CELLS
Each cell has 4 edge bars: TOP=BASS, BOTTOM=HIGH, LEFT=MID, RIGHT=RMS. No signal columns in strip -- signals live on the cells themselves. Playing cells show bars at full opacity; inactive at 30%. Height: ~120px. Speed: vertical column. Signal: 4 cell edge bars.
